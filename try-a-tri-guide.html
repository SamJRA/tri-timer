Setup
The day will start at Mega House at 9am - arrive early! Atheletes must check-in upon arrival and set up their T2 (transition from bike to run) in the garage. A mandatory race briefing will happen inside between 9:30-9:40. At 9:40 athletes will travel to the pool via bike with the option to place other gear in a vehicle that will meet them at the pool. If using the vehicle to transport gear please have everything in a single bag.

🏊 Swim - 750m
The swim will occur during the public lane swim session at Rengstorff pool. The pool will likely be very busy so athletes will select a lane corresponding to their swimming speed and start at the race director's command. If required, athletes will be split into heats to manage lane occupancy. When swimming with 3 or more people in one lane, swimmers must travel in a counterclockwise fashion (always on the right side of the lane) - passing in the middle when required. Athlete must be mindful of other patrons and follow directions of pool staff & lifeguards at all times.
  • The swim distance is 750m (30 laps)
  • Athletes must track their own progress via smart watch or counting laps completed
T1 — Pool to Bike
Athletes will exit the water and make their way outside the recreation complex to transition 1 to start the bike segment. Althetes have the option to stop in the changing room before heading to the bike to use the bathroom or change into biking attire.
Once in transition, athletes must fasten their helmet before leaving T1 onto the bike course.

🚴 Bike — 20km
The bike course is a 20km point-to-point ride from Rengstorff pool to Mega House, extending south through Loyola. This portion is self-supported meaning the athlete must carry their own nutrition and hydration required for the duration of the bike (water bottle highly recommended).
  • Route: ridewithgps.com/routes/55422565
  • See Appendix A for photos of the route
The course will be partially marked with chalk arrows on the ground and small signage. It is the athlete's responsibility to stay on course.
If you're worried about getting lost, carry your phone. Use the free RidewithGPS app with location services turned on to overlay your location on the course map.
The course follows public roads and paths - follow traffic laws and exercise caution at all times!

🏃 Run — 5km
The run consists of a 2 lap clockwise course starting and ending at the Mega House for a total distance of 5km. The course will be partially marked with chalk arrows on the ground.
